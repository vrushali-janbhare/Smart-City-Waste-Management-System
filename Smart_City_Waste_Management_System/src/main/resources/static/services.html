Services for a Cleaner City

E-Safai provides a digital platform that connects citizens, administrators and waste collection workers to improve waste management activities.

📢
01

Waste Reporting

Citizens can report waste-related problems by submitting complaint details, location information and waste images through the online platform.

🚛
02

Waste Collection Management

Administrators can manage reported complaints and assign collection tasks to workers for organized waste collection activities.

🤖
03

AI-Based Waste Classification

AI-based image classification helps identify waste categories from uploaded images and supports better waste management decisions.

📍
04

Complaint Tracking

Citizens can monitor the status of their submitted complaints and stay informed about waste collection progress.

♻
05

Waste Segregation Awareness

Encourage proper waste segregation and responsible disposal practices to support recycling and environmental sustainability.

🏙️
06

Bulk Waste Pickup
